# Initial Brief

The original request that started this project, kept verbatim for
provenance. Everything in [[Glossary]] and the [[Phase-Roadmap]] traces back
to this brief.

> Help me translate the `v` field of `strings.jsonl` into casual Indonesian,
> with the following rules:
> - Don't translate anything related to names.
> - For conversation/dialogue, use casual Indonesian — "gue", "lo", and a
>   casual conversational register.
> - For words that read better in English, keep them in English — e.g.
>   guild, event, login, logout, menu, etc.
>
> Write progress to a handoff document in case token usage runs out or the
> connection drops, and ask if anything is ambiguous.

## How this shaped the project

- "Don't translate names" became the entire "not translated" section of
  [[Glossary]] (character/place/faction/named-skill names).
- "Casual for dialogue, English for loanwords" became the tone rules and the
  English-loanword list in [[Glossary]].
- "Write progress to a handoff document" is the reason a living
  progress-tracking practice existed at all — now formalized as
  [[Current-Status]], [[Session-History]], and [[Resume-Procedure]] instead
  of a single ad hoc file.
