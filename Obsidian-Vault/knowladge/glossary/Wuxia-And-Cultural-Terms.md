# Wuxia & Cultural Terms

Part of [[Glossary]]. Genre-specific vocabulary — wuxia conventions, Traditional Chinese
Medicine (TCM) terms, and native cultural festivals — locked mostly during Phase 1.

## "Sect" → "Sekte"

Standard wuxia term in Indonesian translation, **translated** (not kept in English).
Example: "Sect Rules Violation Notice" → "Pemberitahuan Pelanggaran Aturan Sekte".

**Exception**: "Mohist Sect" (the sect/order at Hidden Mountain) is kept fully in English as
a proper-noun faction name — see [[Names-Not-Translated]]. *(Phase 1; exception locked
[[Phase-9]] batch 5)*

## Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) terms

In illness/medicine lore: general concepts (qi stagnation, damp-heat, etc.) are translated
descriptively ("stagnasi qi", "lembap-panas"), but **named diseases** (Shegong's Disease,
Huhuo Syndrome, Wind-Damp Bi Syndrome) keep their core name (Shegong, Huhuo, Bi) while the
generic word is translated ("Syndrome" → "Sindrom", "Disease" → "Penyakit"). *(Phase 1)*

## Native cultural festivals

Translated into descriptive Indonesian (not treated as proper nouns): "Spring Festival" →
"Festival Musim Semi", "Double Ninth Festival" → "Festival Sembilan Ganda". *(Phase 1)*

- **"Lantern Festival" → "Festival Lampion"**, consistent with the others. *([[Phase-9]])*

## Other locked cultural terms

- **"Red Envelope" / lunar-new-year-style lucky gift → "Angpao"** (the common Indonesian
  term for this Chinese New Year tradition). *(Phase 4)*
- **Seasons in effect/buff text (Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter) are translated** as Musim
  Semi/Musim Panas/Musim Gugur/Musim Dingin when used as a descriptive seasonal-effect label
  (not a titled feature name). *(Phase 4)*
- **Chinese zodiac double-hour ("X Hour"/"X hour") → "Jam X"** (e.g. "Hai Hour" → "Jam Hai",
  "Xu hour" → "Jam Xu") — the hour name (Hai, Xu, etc.) is kept in English/Pinyin as a
  traditional time term with no standard equivalent; only the word "Hour" is translated.
  *(Phase 5 closing / Phase 6 opening, idx 49000–50999)*
- **"Form" as a transformation state** (Carp form, Wind form, Vulpine Form, Feline Form) →
  **"Wujud"** (e.g. "Vulpine Form" → "Wujud Vulpine"). *(Phase 5 closing / Phase 6 opening,
  idx 49000–50999)*

## "Trial" — cutover note

**"Trial"** → "Uji Coba" was used consistently for challenge-mode/dungeon trials early on,
but a later precedent (around idx ~35000+) started keeping `"Trial: <Name>"` titles fully in
English instead. See [[Phase-5]] for the exact cutover point before applying either rule to
a new occurrence. *(Phase 1 → cutover in Phase 5)*
