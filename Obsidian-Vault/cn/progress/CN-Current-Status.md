Indonesian translation automatically (the **bridge**, `tools/bridge.py` — 1,062,051 of
2,081,685 `zh_cn` entries share a key with global, and 196,495 more reuse the global
translation of the same Mandarin text). Only the **difference** below — text never translated
in global — is translated here, from the Mandarin text.

never edit them by hand. CI fails a PR whose block is stale (`--profile cn --check`).
